About us

UNDER Water is a diving club for all people. Every week, we organize large-scale trips with everyone to the most fascinating places on the planet to visit its water world.

We were founded in Sunny Odesa in Ukraine, but we are expanding our club and meeting people from all over the world, so you can make friends too!

You can book or buy a trip right away and go explore this world with professionals who will help you. You just need to book a call and know all the details.

Let’s talk about some important things if you’re new to diving

01 Who can join the diving club?

Everyone! We have coaches who train children as well as adults and even older ones! No prior experience needed.

02 Can I join the club from another country?

Yes, absolutely! We welcome members from all over the world and can help with arrangements and online consultations.

03 Should I buy my own diving gear?

It's not necessary to start. We offer high-quality gear rental for all our tours and courses. You can buy your own later.

04 What are the health requirements for diving?

Generally, you need to be in good physical health. A medical questionnaire will be provided before the first dive.

05 What to do so that the ears do not hurt?

Our instructors will teach you equalization techniques (popping your ears) to prevent pain and injury during descent.

06 I wear glasses. Can I go diving?

Yes! You can use contact lenses or rent a special mask with prescription lenses.

07 What are the prices for tours?

Prices vary depending on the location and duration. Check our 'Pricing' section or contact us for a custom quote.
